# Wrapping Up

You've analyzed four games that I built, and then you built a small game with your own hands.
That means you already have a working mental model for *any* JetLag game, not just the ones in this book.
Every chapter from here on goes deep on one piece of that model: rigid body physics next, then movement, then gesture input, and so on.
For the most part, the rest of the chapters won't ask you to use a feature before it is introduced.

Your `unit_01` and `firefly_jar` folders don't need to go anywhere.
Leave them in your repository and move on.
The next chapter will start by asking you to make a new folder, with a new name.
