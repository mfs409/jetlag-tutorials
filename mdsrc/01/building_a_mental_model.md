# Building a Mental Model

A game is two things at once.
It's a simulation, running according to rules you set up in advance.
It's also a play, watched by an audience who expects to be entertained.
Neither view tells the whole story by itself.
The simulation explains *why* something happens, and the play explains why it *matters* that it happened.

JetLag gives you two levers, the **Camera** and **Gravity**, that reshape both views at once.
Change one of them, and the same handful of actors can look like an overhead arena, a side-scrolling platformer, or a vertical climb.
